Hi, I'm **Carlos Velasquez**—an Electronic Engineer specializing in embedded systems, real-time firmware development, and hardware-software integration. 

I focus on building reliable, resource-constrained systems, with expertise spanning low-level driver development, Real-Time Operating Systems (Zephyr, FreeRTOS), and end-to-end IoT product design.

---

### Core Technical Stack

* **Firmware & Software:** Embedded C, C++, Zephyr RTOS, FreeRTOS, Python, Git.
* **Microcontrollers & Platforms:** Nordic nRF52840 (PAN1780), ESP32 / ESP32-S3, PAN9520, Raspberry Pi, STM32, Arduino, etc.
* **Peripherals & Protocols:** I2C, SPI, UART, DMA, BLE, Wi-Fi, NFC, PWM, sensor bus architectures.
* **Hardware & Prototyping:** Custom PCB design & schematic capture, signal integrity debugging (oscilloscopes, logic analyzers), 3D CAD modeling & rapid prototyping.

### What You'll Find On This Blog

This site serves as a technical notebook and engineering portfolio. I publish hands-on post-mortems and technical breakdowns covering:

1. **Firmware Architecture & Drivers:** Writing modular, non-blocking drivers (I2C/SPI) for ToF sensors, IMUs, power monitors, and LED controllers.
2. **DSP & Sensor Processing:** Implementing fixed-point noise filters (Moving Average, Kalman, IIR) to clean up noisy sensor telemetry.
3. **Hardware & Rapid Prototyping:** Enclosure design, physical hardware builds, and component integration using custom electronics and 3D printing.
4. **RTOS Design Patterns:** Thread synchronization, interrupt service routines (ISRs), power management, and memory optimization in C/C++.
